    Words to use correctly

    Posters that use these accurately read as confident. Check each one against your own poster.

    Ore
    Rock that contains enough of a metal compound to be worth mining.
    Reduction
    Loss of oxygen from a compound, or gain of electrons by an ion.
    Smelting
    Heating an ore with a reducing agent, usually carbon, to get the metal.
    Electrolysis
    Using electricity to break down a molten or dissolved ionic compound.
    Electrolyte
    The molten or dissolved compound that carries current during electrolysis.
    Cathode
    The negative electrode. Metal ions gain electrons here.
    Anode
    The positive electrode. Ions lose electrons here.
    Slag
    Waste made when impurities in an ore combine with limestone or silica in a furnace.
    Alloy
    A mixture of a metal with other elements, usually stronger than the pure metal.
