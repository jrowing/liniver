Topics & new game

How to play: every green tile in a connected province earns 🪙1 per turn; units cost upkeep. A unit captures a tile only if its strength beats the tile's defence (towns defend neighbours at 1, castles at 2, units at their level). Drop a unit onto a friendly one to merge them. Destroy every rival town to win. Answer a physics question each turn to bank bonus coins.
