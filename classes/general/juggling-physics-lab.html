How siteswap works

Each digit is how many beats later that throw lands. Hands alternate, right hand on even beats, so an odd throw crosses to the other hand and an even throw returns to the same hand.

The average of the digits is the number of balls. A 0 is an empty hand and a 2 is a held ball. Letters a to f stand for 10 to 15. The state shows which of the next beats already have a ball arriving.

Physics

Catches happen at this distance from the centre line, throws from about a third of it.
